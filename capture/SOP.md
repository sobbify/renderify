# FIELD CAPTURE / SOP

This is the field contract for a useful indoor twin. Record the place as a measurement, not as a cinematic montage.

## Before recording

- Confirm permission and the source footage licence.
- Clean the lens and lock focus/exposure if the phone allows it.
- Prefer a bright, stable space with visible texture.
- Walk a connected loop or a corridor with overlap.

## While recording

Hold the phone at a consistent height. Walk slowly. Pan only as needed. Keep motion blur low, avoid covering the lens, and leave enough overlap between adjacent views. Capture doors, corners, floor transitions, and the route you expect a viewer to use.

## After recording

Keep the original file, note duration and dimensions, and record the source licence. Run the frame preflight before sending the clip to the reconstruction pipeline. Do not describe an area as measured until the QA report supports it.

## Principle

A boring, steady capture beats a dramatic one. The output should be honest about what the camera saw and silent about what it did not.
